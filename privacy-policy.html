MAGMA STUDIOS / PUZZLE WORLD

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026 · Last updated: November 17, 2026

Puzzle World is a free game developed by Magma Studios. You can play without an account, advertisements, or in-app purchases.

Your game. Your privacy.
We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data through Puzzle World.

Information collection and use

Puzzle World does not collect personal or sensitive user data. It does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or advertising identifier. No account or sign-in is required.

Advertising, analytics, and third parties

The game contains no advertisements, in-app purchases, analytics services, tracking tools, or third-party SDKs that collect user data. Magma Studios does not share or sell player data.

If you download the game through Google Play, Google may process information independently to operate its store. Those activities are governed by Google’s Privacy Policy.

Data storage, retention, and deletion

Magma Studios does not receive or store gameplay data or personal data from Puzzle World on its servers. Game state used during play stays on your device. You can remove any app data retained on your device through Android’s app settings or by uninstalling the game. There is no online account to delete.

Data security

Because Puzzle World does not transmit personal data to us, there is no player database or personal data transfer for Magma Studios to secure. Data held on your device is subject to your device’s security protections.

Children’s privacy

Puzzle World does not collect personal information from any player, including children. It has no accounts, advertisements, or tracking.

Contacting us

For questions about this policy, contact Magma Studios at fohlan@outlook.com.

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever information you choose to include. We use that information only to respond to and resolve your inquiry, retain it only as long as needed for that purpose or as required by law, and do not sell it or use it for marketing. Email is handled through our email provider, Microsoft Outlook. You can request deletion of your correspondence at the same address, subject to any legal retention requirements.

Changes to this policy

We will publish any changes on this page and update the date above. If a future version of Puzzle World changes its data practices, this policy will be updated to describe those practices.
